## Our commitment

We are committed to providing an environment in which everyone can participate in
this project safely and respectfully, regardless of experience, identity, or
background. This Code of Conduct applies to the repository's issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Expected behavior

- Address technical ideas with specific evidence and alternatives, not judgments about people.
- Assume good intent around questions and mistakes, and provide enough context for newcomers to understand.
- Make review feedback clear and actionable while respecting differing views.
- Do not disclose personal information or private conversations without permission.
- Acknowledge and correct mistakes, and seek the outcome that best serves the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, threats, harassment, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention and imagery
- Deliberate provocation, repeated derailing, spam, or other conduct that obstructs collaboration
- Disclosure of another person's private information, credentials, or confidential material
- Copyright infringement, falsified attribution, malicious code, or intentionally dangerous changes
- Repeated refusal to honor a reasonable request to stop or a maintainer's moderation decision

## Enforcement

Report an incident privately to the repository owner whenever possible. If no
private contact channel is available, open an issue containing no sensitive
details and ask how to continue privately. Include the links, time, and description
needed to assess the incident while minimizing sensitive information. The
repository owner may investigate and, as appropriate, issue a warning, edit or
remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or impose a permanent ban.

The privacy of reporters and involved parties will be protected as far as
practical. Retaliation against a good-faith report is prohibited. Anyone with a
clear conflict of interest must not take part in deciding that matter.
